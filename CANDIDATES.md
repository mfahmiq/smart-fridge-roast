## Tournament (single elimination, pairwise, brief criteria in weight order)

### Round 1
- 1 vs 2 → **1**. Both on-gadget, one line. #1's hostage proof-of-life image is fresher and the sting lands harder than #2's chair-leg vendetta, which leans on the familiar vacuum-bumps-things trope (criteria 2, 3).
- 3 vs 4 → **3**. #3's Despacito mishearing turns on a real speaker behaviour and ends on the accent twist; #4's 3 a.m. sauna is funny but a known thermostat-at-night trope (criteria 2, 3).
- 5 vs 6 → **5**. #5's "everyone except the burglar" is a sharper, single-turn sting; #6's passive-aggressive roommate is relatable but softer (criterion 2).
- 7 vs 8 → **7**. #7 is a double-turn with a real behaviour (firmware lockout); #8's "admin for darkness" is a good sting but one turn only (criteria 2, 5).
- 9 vs 10 → **10**. #9's judging fridge is stock-adjacent; #10's toddler-with-a-Lego image is vivid and unmet (criteria 2, 3).
- 11 vs 12 → **11**. Both land; #11's football-results announcement is the fresher image versus #12's diva, a more familiar metaphor (criterion 3).
- 13 vs 14 → **14**. #13's mother-in-law credit card is funny, but #14's "either haunted or just bad at its one job" is the harder sting (criterion 2).
- 15 vs 16 → **16**. #15 is a mild observation with a weak sting; #16's Wi-Fi paperweight with abandonment issues is fresher and funnier (criteria 2, 3).

### Round 2
- 1 vs 3 → **3**. Both use hostage imagery; #3's sting lands at the very end on the accent twist and its setup is tighter, while #1 spends more of the line on setup (criteria 2, 4).
- 5 vs 7 → **7**. #5 has one sharp turn; #7 compounds the irony — locked out by your own lock, then the rescue email is unreachable inside (criterion 2).
- 10 vs 11 → **10**. #10's toddler-Lego image is more vivid and "regrets everything" lands harder than #11's football-results comparison (criterion 2).
- 14 vs 16 → **14**. Both name real behaviours; #14's ending is punchier and its expiry-alert behaviour is more specifically a fridge behaviour than a kettle needing Wi-Fi (criteria 2, 5).

### Semi-finals
- 3 vs 7 → **7**. #3 has one great turn; #7 has two turns in a self-defeating loop, a bigger laugh (criterion 2).
- 10 vs 14 → **10**. #10's image is funnier and fresher than #14's haunted-yoghurt premise (criteria 2, 3).

### Final
- 7 vs 10 → **7 (CHAMPION)**. Criterion 1: both roast a smart home gadget in one line — tie. Criterion 2: #7's laugh is stronger — the sting is a self-defeating loop with two turns (locked out, then the rescue is inside the house), and the final clause "because my phone was inside" is a perfect ironic kicker; #10 has one vivid image. Criterion 3: both fresh, but a firmware update locking you out is rarer in jokes than a vacuum mishap. #7 wins on criteria 2 and 3.
